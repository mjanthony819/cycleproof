Where to leave room

The limit is an illustrative planning input. Rental events are not simultaneous inventory. No staffing ratio or fleet size is inferred.

What was known?

Calendar, the count exactly 24 hours earlier, and the count exactly 168 hours earlier.

Day-ahead protocol: each target day's lags are known by the start of the target day. No target-day weather, casual or registered component counts.

Dates use Washington local calendar labels. No timezone conversion is applied to hourly CSV labels.
Missing lag? No invented zero. The row is excluded. Some dates have fewer than 24 eligible hours; do not treat their totals as a full day.

A fixed depth-3 tree is retrained before each 2012 quarter, compared with the weekly baseline. This is a separate stability check, not the final fixed-model test.

Neural reproducibility is not one lucky seed

Seed 7 was predeclared for the app. Seeds 19 and 31 are sensitivity runs, not selected after test performance. Two hidden ReLU layers, 48 and 24 units; not a temporal CNN or a pretrained model.

Your historical scenario / strict schema

Check the input before
you trust the output.

Import up to 744 historical target hours. Use lag counts from exactly the same hour one day and one week before. Import is local and never uploaded.

1. Prepare CSV

Plain unquoted UTF-8 CSV, exact column order. Dates must be in 2011-2012. No target, weather or count-component columns accepted.

2. Review the contract

  • hour: integer 0-23.
  • workingday and holiday: 0 or 1, no weekend or holiday marked working.
  • lag24 and lag168: nonnegative hourly counts, maximum 1,000 as a guardrail.
  • Unique target hours, valid dates, all values required.
  • Numeric checks do not prove lags are truthful or available at forecast time.
This model was trained on one historical city. Passing validation is not proof of suitability for current data, another city or a station.

Intended use

Educational day-ahead demand replay and model comparison for students and community-mobility planners. Explore uncertainty before deciding whether more validation is worth doing.

Not intended for

Live inventory, safety decisions, automatic staffing, station allocation, financial commitments or claims about transport demand today.

Known failure modes

  • Growth, holidays and disruptions break yesterday/week patterns.
  • 165 missing timestamps; lag eligibility excludes additional rows. Evaluation is conditional on complete lags, not all hours.
  • October 29-30 has just one eligible row. This dataset cannot support a storm-performance claim.
  • September-calibrated intervals may under-cover the later test months.
  • System demand hides neighborhood inequity and station shortages.
  • Historical rentals are served demand, not unmet demand.

How it was built

Built during November 2026 for Peninsula Innovation Hackathon. AI assistance was used for concept development, code, tests, documentation and narration. Models were actually trained from scratch on the credited dataset using CPU scikit-learn. Browser inference is a transparent JavaScript reimplementation of the exported boosted trees and neural weights. No paid API, fake predictions, external inference service or hidden live data.
